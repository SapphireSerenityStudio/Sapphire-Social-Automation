Privacy Policy — Sapphire Social Automation

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Sapphire Social Automation is a personal content automation tool used only by its owner to manage and republish their own social media content.

The application may access Pinterest account information necessary to read boards and Pins selected by the account owner. This access is used only to support automated publishing workflows, such as sending approved content to the owner’s Instagram account.

Sapphire Social Automation does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

The application does not collect Pinterest passwords or login credentials. Authentication is handled through Pinterest’s official authorization process.

Any data temporarily stored for automation purposes, such as Pinterest Pin IDs used to prevent duplicate posts, is used only to operate the workflow.

This application is for private, personal use and is not offered as a public service to other users.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact Sapphire through the SapphireSerenityStudio GitHub profile.
